Who I Am

A striker in the making

I'm Goodluck Okpro, a striker from Delta State, Nigeria, currently a student at Faith Academy, Warri. Football has been my passion since I first stepped onto a school pitch — I live for the moment the ball hits the back of the net.

I joined my school football team in JSS2 and have since scored 4 goals across multiple matches. In June 2026, I took the next step in my journey by joining a football academy, where I train every day to sharpen my game and grow closer to my dream of becoming a professional footballer.

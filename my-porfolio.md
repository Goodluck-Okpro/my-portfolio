# ⚽ Precious Uba - Personal Portfolio Website

## Project Overview

This project is a modern, responsive personal portfolio website for **Precious Uba**, a young Nigerian footballer.

The website serves as a digital portfolio that showcases who I am, my football journey, my interests, achievements, and personal gallery. It should feel professional, modern, and inspiring—similar to the personal websites of professional athletes, but designed for a young player at the beginning of his career.

This website is intended to grow over time as I progress in football.

---

# Project Goals

The website should:

- Introduce visitors to who I am.
- Showcase my football journey.
- Display my interests and hobbies.
- Share my match records and achievements.
- Present a professional online presence.
- Allow visitors to contact me.
- Be easy to expand with future achievements.

# Project Vision

This website is more than a school project or personal page—it is intended to become my long-term digital portfolio as I develop my football career. Every design and technical decision should support that vision. The site should be easy to update with new photos, match statistics, achievements, and milestones over the coming years.
